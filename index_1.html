FAQ

Your Questions. Answered.

Everything you need to know about buying and selling on CredSwap.

What is CredSwap?
CredSwap is a peer-to-peer marketplace for AI and cloud credits. We connect startups and developers who need credits at scale with sellers who have surplus — enabling significant savings vs. retail pricing from Anthropic, OpenAI, AWS, and Azure.
Which credits are available and how much can I save?
We currently offer Anthropic Claude API, OpenAI API, AWS, and Azure OpenAI credits. Buyers typically save 40–60% vs. retail pricing depending on the provider and volume. Contact us for current availability and exact pricing.
Will rate limits or performance change after buying?
No. Credits are on real, verified accounts with the same rate limits as any direct account at that tier. OpenAI accounts are Tier 5; Anthropic and AWS accounts come with enterprise rate limits.
How do I pay when buying? What about selling?
Buyers pay via UPI or direct bank transfer (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS) — simple and traceable. Sellers receive payment in cryptocurrency (BTC, USDT, ETH) for full anonymity and fast cross-border settlement.
Can I verify the account before paying?
Yes. We provide balance screenshots and can optionally share a read-only API key so you can run a test call and confirm the credit balance and rate limits before any money changes hands.
Will my identity be shared?
No. Both parties sign a mutual NDA and identities remain confidential throughout. CredSwap acts as the intermediary. Only basic entity and tax details are shared for invoicing purposes.
What if credits become inaccessible?
If access becomes unavailable within the stated validity period, CredSwap will replace the credits from another verified seller, or refund the unused balance after verification of the issue.
Where do the credits come from?
Credits come from verified sellers — typically developers, early-stage startups, or accelerator program recipients with surplus credits they don't intend to use. Every seller is vetted by CredSwap before listing.
